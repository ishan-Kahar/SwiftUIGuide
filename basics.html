2. Spacer aur layout: constraints ki jagah

SwiftUI ka default rule: har view utni hi jagah leta hai jitni use chahiye, aur poora group screen ke beech mein aata hai. Spacer() bachi hui saari jagah le leta hai aur cheezon ko kinare dhakel deta hai. Neeche ek hi VStack ke chaar version hain; gulabi dashed area Spacer hai.

Rule: ek stack mein jitne Spacer, bachi hui jagah utne barabar hisson mein bat jaati hai. HStack mein bhi yahi hota hai: HStack { Text("Left"); Spacer(); Text("Right") }
VStack(spacing: 20)Har child ke beech 20pt. Default lagbhag 8pt hota hai.
Spacer().frame(height: 40)Fixed 40pt gap, jaise constant constraint.
.padding(.horizontal, 24)Left aur right margin 24pt.
.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)Leading/trailing = 0. Full width.
VStack(alignment: .leading)Andar sab left aligned.
.ignoresSafeArea()Background notch ke peeche tak.
